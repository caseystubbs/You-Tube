# What Actually Happened in My Account This Week

MUU closed for $326, two SPY spreads added $697, PL and OKLO were assigned, and Adobe is still open.

I collected $1,350 in option premium this week, but that is not the same thing as saying I made $1,350. The closed trades tell a different story, and one trade I initially left out of the recap matters a lot: MUU.

The MUU Oct. 16 $26 put position was opened on Sept. 1 at $3.15 and closed on Sept. 8 at $1.52. With two contracts, that produced $326 in realized profit. Add that to the two SPY spreads that closed for $272 and $425, and the realized profit from those closed trades comes to $1,023.

At the same time, PL and OKLO were assigned, which was an accepted outcome because I was willing to own the shares. Adobe remains open, so its $1,040 in collected premium is still attached to an active obligation.

When I review a week like this, I care about the dollars, but I also care about whether the decisions deserve to be repeated. A profitable trade can still come from poor process, while an assignment can be exactly what I wanted if I sold the put because I was genuinely willing to own the stock.
